# Superstore Sales Analysis

## 📊 Project Overview

This project analyzes Superstore sales data using **Microsoft Excel and SQL** to identify sales trends, profitability, customer segments, regional performance, product performance, and the impact of discounts.

## 🛠️ Tools Used

- Microsoft Excel
- SQL
- Google BigQuery
- Pivot Tables
- Excel Charts
- Data Analysis

## 📌 Key Business Findings

- Technology generated the highest sales and profit among the three categories.
- Furniture had high sales but a comparatively low profit margin of about 2%.
- The West region generated the highest sales and profit.
- Sales increased overall from 2014 to 2017.
- Higher discount levels were generally associated with lower average profit.
- Technology had the highest average profit per unit.
- Tables, Bookcases, and Supplies were loss-making sub-categories.

## 📈 Dashboard

The Excel dashboard presents:

- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Total Quantity
- Average Discount
- Sales by Category
- Profit by Category
- Sales by Region
- Profit by Region
- Sales by Customer Segment
- Sales and Profit Trends
